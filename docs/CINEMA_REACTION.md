# Cinema ticket reaction

Sabi uses 🎟️ for explicit cinema, showtimes and film requests. This rule takes priority over generic request words such as "quiero" and embedded greetings. Thanks and complaints keep their own reactions. No random rotation with 🎬 is added.

The emoji acknowledges the topic. It does not mean showtimes were found, tickets were reserved, or a purchase was made. Reaction requests are best effort: provider acceptance does not prove visible placement on WhatsApp.

This change does not enable a processing 🧠 reaction, waiting notices, games, wider memory, or reaction removal. Those remain separate proposals.
